Frequently Asked Questions

Is freepage.app really free?

Yes, freepage.app is 100% free to use. There are no hidden fees, no premium tiers required for basic functionality, and no ads. All core document writing features including rich text formatting, multiple document management, auto-save, and export to TXT, Markdown, HTML, PDF, and DOCX are completely free.

Can I use freepage.app offline?

Yes. After your first visit, freepage.app caches all necessary files using a service worker. You can continue writing, editing, and managing documents even without an internet connection. All data is stored locally in your browser.

Where are my documents saved?

All documents are saved to your browser's localStorage. This means your documents never leave your device and are never sent to any server. They persist between sessions as long as you don't clear your browser data.

Can I export my documents to Word?

Yes, freepage.app supports direct export to .docx format, which can be opened in Microsoft Word, Google Docs, and other word processors. freepage.app also supports export to plain text (.txt), Markdown (.md), HTML (.html), and PDF (.pdf) formats. All formatting including headings, bold, italic, underline, and lists is preserved in the .docx export.

What happens if I clear my browser data?

If you clear your browser's localStorage, your documents will be deleted. To protect your work, regularly export important documents using the export feature. freepage.app recommends exporting backups of critical documents to your local file system.

Does freepage.app work on mobile?

Yes, freepage.app is fully responsive and works on mobile devices including phones and tablets. The interface adapts to smaller screens with a slide-out sidebar, collapsible panels, and touch-friendly controls.
